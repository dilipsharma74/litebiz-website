What LiteBiz ERP does

Six modules, one single source of truth

Purchasing & suppliers

Purchase orders, receiving, supplier history and landed costs, all in one place.

Multi-location inventory

Real-time stock across warehouses, outlets and branches — no more separate spreadsheets.

Sales & point of sale

Fast counter and wholesale invoicing, with stock updated the instant a sale happens.

Full financials

Cash flow, receivables, payables and reconciliation — without re-keying anything twice.
